# paint2print Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

**paint2print does not collect, store or share any personal data.** Everything the add-in does happens on your own computer.

## 1. Who we are

paint2print is an add-in for Autodesk Fusion that lets you colour 3D models and send them to your slicer. It is published by Bauke Plugge, Netherlands.

Questions about privacy: **bauke@plugge.eu**.

## 2. Data we collect and how it is used

We collect no data. paint2print has no account, analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting, and it sends nothing over the internet to us or to anyone else.

To do its job, the add-in works only with the following, on your own computer:

- the model open in Fusion and the colours you assign to it;
- the file it creates for your slicer, saved in C:\Users\<you>\AppData\Local\Temp\Paint2Print\;
- its own settings, such as the location of your slicer, stored in C:\Users\<you>\AppData\Roaming\Paint2Print\.

paint2print uses this only to colour your model and hand the file to the slicer installed on your computer. None of it is sent to us, and we cannot see it. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

Autodesk Fusion and your slicer are separate programs from other companies, with their own privacy policies. paint2print does not change what they collect.

## 3. Third parties and affiliates

We share no data with third parties, because paint2print collects none. The add-in contains no analytics tools, advertising networks or third-party SDKs that collect data. Bakue Plugge has no parent company, subsidiaries or related entities that receive data.

If this ever changes, we will update this policy before the change ships. Any third party or affiliate that then receives data from paint2print must protect it in the same way as this policy describes.

You get paint2print from the Autodesk App Store, which Autodesk runs under its own privacy statement. 

## 4. Data retention and deletion

We store no data about you, so there is nothing for us to keep or delete. The files and settings paint2print creates stay on your computer until you delete them. If you email us, we keep the conversation for 12 months after your question is answered, then delete it.

## 5. Revoking consent and requesting deletion

paint2print needs no consent, because it collects no data. To remove everything it has stored on your computer:

1. In Fusion, open **Utilities → Add-Ins → Scripts and Add-Ins**, stop paint2print, and uninstall it: e.g. Windows Settings → Apps, or the uninstaller that came with it.
2. Delete the slicer files it created in C:\Users\<you>\AppData\Local\Temp\Paint2Print\.
3. Delete its settings in C:\Users\<you>\AppData\Roaming\Paint2Print\.

To have your emails with us deleted, or for any other privacy question, email bauke@plugge.eu. We act on requests within one month. You can also complain to the data protection authority in your country; in the Netherlands, that is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

## 6. Changes and contact

If paint2print ever changes how it handles data, we will update this policy and the date at the top before that version is released.
